FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some common questions.

Are you affiliated with Tesla? +

No. Tesla Auto Connect is an independent vehicle marketplace and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tesla, Inc.

Can I request a specific Tesla model? +

Yes. You can select your preferred vehicle model through the vehicle request form.

Are the listed vehicles always available? +

Availability can change. Vehicle information, pricing and availability should be confirmed before placing an order.

Do you offer delivery? +

Delivery arrangements may be available depending on the vehicle and destination. Delivery details and applicable costs are confirmed separately.

Does submitting a request mean I have purchased a vehicle? +

No. Submitting the form is a vehicle request only. A request does not automatically create a completed vehicle purchase or delivery agreement.

How can I contact support? +

You can contact our support team by email or submit a vehicle request through the website.
